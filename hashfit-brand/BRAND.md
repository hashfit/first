# HASHFIT brand kit

## The logo idea

The mark is a hashtag (`#`) built from two slanted uprights and two crossbars, so it reads as both a **#** (HASH-fit) and a bold italic **H**. The lower crossbar is red and sticks out a bit further left than the top one, which gives it forward motion.

## Colours

| Name | Hex | RGB | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| HASHFIT Red | `#E8312E` | 232, 49, 46 | The accent. The lower bar of the mark, taglines, AFTER tags, CTAs. Keep it to about 10% of any design. |
| Black | `#0A0A0A` | 10, 10, 10 | The main background. |
| Bone | `#F4F1EC` | 244, 241, 236 | Logo and headline colour on black. A warmer alternative to pure white. |
| Ash | `#8A847C` | 138, 132, 124 | Secondary text and small print. |
| Charcoal | `#1C1A19` | 28, 26, 25 | Cards and panels on top of black. |

## Fonts (all free on Google Fonts)

- **Anton** for the wordmark, headlines and numbers (for example "-9KG IN 12 WEEKS"). Always uppercase.
- **Inter ExtraBold** for taglines and labels. Uppercase with wide letter spacing (about 0.3em).
- **Inter Regular/Medium** for body text.
- **Instrument Serif Italic** as an optional accent for a single emphasised word, in red.

## Files

Every file is in `svg/` (scalable, for print or any size) and `png/`.

Every asset comes in a **dark** version (black background, off-white logo) and a **light** version (off-white background, black logo). The logos also come with no background at all (`transparent-white` for dark photos, `transparent-black` for light ones).

## Rules

- On black, use a bone or white logo. On light backgrounds, use the black logo. The red bar stays red either way.
- Leave clear space around the logo equal to the height of one crossbar.
- Don't stretch it, recolour the red bar, add shadows or glows, or put it on busy photos without the badge behind it.
- The logo should be at least 24px tall on screen.
