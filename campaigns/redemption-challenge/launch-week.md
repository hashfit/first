# Redemption Challenge launch week

## The plan (this bit is for you, not for posting)

- Marketing runs Sunday 1 Nov to Friday 6 Nov. Doors close Friday at midnight.
- I've assumed the challenge starts Monday 9 Nov, so the weekend is onboarding. Change it if you've got a different start day.
- Every CTA is "DM me REDEMPTION" (or comment it). With one keyword you can see straight away who came from launch week. If you're using a checkout link, swap it for "link in bio".

## DM reply when someone sends REDEMPTION

Don't send the link straight away. Have a quick chat first so you know where they're at.

1. "Yo bro, appreciate you reaching out. Before I send you the details, what made you message? What's going on for you right now?"
2. Once they reply: "Respect for being honest about that bro. And what would you want to be different by the end of the 8 weeks?"
3. Then: "Sick, that's exactly what this is built for. Here's everything." (send the details or link)
4. If they go quiet, follow up once the next day: "Hey bro, just checking you got this. Doors close Friday night."
